<p>Oscanner turns paper into clean, shareable PDFs and pictures, entirely on your phone.</p>

<p><b>Scan</b></p>
<ul>
<li>The camera finds the page and takes the picture when you hold steady</li>
<li>Crop with a magnifier, straighten, rotate and flatten curved pages: lines made level and text crowded into a book's spine spread back out</li>
<li>Looks: Auto, Bright, Gray, Ink (pure black and white), Board for whiteboards and Photo for old prints; shadows are removed</li>
<li>Modes for ID cards (printed at true size), open books (split into two pages), whiteboards, slides and screens</li>
<li>Read QR codes and barcodes; nothing opens unless you tap it</li>
<li>Import photos, PDFs, and Word, Excel and PowerPoint files you already have</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Text and translation</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Reads the text in your scans, so you can search inside them and copy words out</li>
<li>Searchable PDFs, plain text, Word (.docx) that keeps the layout, Excel tables and PowerPoint slides</li>
<li>Translate a document into 58 languages on the phone, as text or over the page's own picture; each language is downloaded once</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Documents</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Documents named for what they are (receipt, letter, invoice…), folders, search, tags and a Trash that keeps things for 30 days</li>
<li>Merge, reorder, rotate, copy out and delete pages</li>
<li>Sign, write, highlight and add text on pages; remove handwriting, fingers, stains and show-through</li>
<li>Black out private details, also removed from the text</li>
<li>Test papers: split into one question a page, mark the ones you got wrong and gather them to practise</li>
<li>Share as PDF, a smaller PDF, pictures or one long picture; send to a computer on the same Wi-Fi; save to any app that keeps files, or print</li>
<li>Password-protected PDFs (AES-256), passport and ID photos, your own watermark</li>
<li>Lock the app with your fingerprint, face or screen lock</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Private by design</b>: no account, no ads, no tracking and no watermark of its own. Scans never leave your phone unless you share them. The only network use is downloading a text-reading or translation language you ask for.</p>
